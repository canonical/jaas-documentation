(manage-access-to-a-controller)=
## Manage access to a controller

**Grant access to a controller.** Given an entity A and a controller, to grant A access to the controller, add a relation between A and the controller, specifying the desired access level.

> See more: {ref}`add-a-relation`

**Revoke access to a controller.** Given an entity A and a controller, to revoke A's access to the controller, remove the relation that grants that access.

> See more: {ref}`remove-a-relation`
